# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Superstore sales data using Power BI and create an interactive dashboard that helps identify sales performance, profitability, regional trends, customer segments, and product-level performance.

The dashboard provides interactive filters for Region, Order Date, and Category to support business-focused analysis and storytelling.

## Key Dashboard Insights

### 1. Sales Performance
The dashboard provides an overall view of sales performance through the Total Sales KPI and the Sales by Category, Segment, Region, and Sub-Category visuals.

### 2. Profitability
The Total Profit KPI and Profit by Region and Profit by Sub-Category charts help identify which areas contribute positively to business profitability.

### 3. Regional Performance
Sales and profit are compared across Central, East, South, and West regions to identify regional differences in business performance.

### 4. Product Performance
Category and Sub-Category analysis provides a detailed view of which product groups generate higher sales and profit.

### 5. Customer Segments
Sales are analyzed across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments to understand the contribution of different customer groups.

### 6. Time-Based Trends
The sales trend visual helps identify changes in sales performance over the order period.

### 7. Interactive Analysis
Region, Order Date, and Category slicers allow users to dynamically filter the dashboard and explore specific parts of the dataset.

The dashboard combines KPIs, charts, trends, and interactive filters to make sales and profitability patterns easier to understand and communicate.

The project provides a practical example of data visualization and storytelling for business decision-making.
